# Runner

`<cli> runner poll` (no model) starts one headless `drive --next` session per poll (its `-next` claim admits one at a time, and none while one holds it) with drive's model and effort when ready work exists, backs off on low GraphQL quota, and releases the claim of a crashed or hung session; `runner status` prints its health as JSON. The runner has no scheduler or queue logic of its own: drive keeps every rule.

Cleanup: each poll and `drive` first removes settled worktrees (merged or closed PR, or head in the base; changes backed up), ignoring the desktop app's VM mount. Under 50 GB free disk drive starts no new worktree and the runner no parallel session, saying why.

Unit: automatic runs take an epic, not a story. An open story or bug under an open parent epic is never a target on its own; its epic is, ranked by the best rank of itself and its open slices, once, and the session keeps driving it (`continue: drive <epic>` after each finished slice) until the epic is done or drive returns an `owner` or `blocked` step. Exceptions: an issue carrying the base-red marker is still taken first, by itself, and an open story or bug with no open parent epic (an orphan) is taken on its own. The runner starts no second session for an epic while a live session holds its claim or any slice's claim.

Order: `drive --next` and the runner take ready work by one rank, ties by issue number: (1) an issue that keeps main or the base red (body carries `<!-- workflow:base-red -->`), (2) `state:started`, (3) `type:bug`, (4) the rest. `lane:owner` issues and the Owner inbox are never taken; owner-waiting issues stay last.

Trusted authors only: anyone may raise an issue, but `drive --next`, the runner and `drive N` take an open issue only when its author is the owner, implementer or reviewer login, or its timeline shows label `owner:approved` applied by the owner login (the newest label event decides). A missing author or an unreadable timeline fails closed, and `drive N` returns an `owner` step asking the owner to label it. Removing the label withdraws approval; editing the body after the label withdraws approval (GraphQL `lastEditedAt` later than the label event; an unreadable edit time fails closed).

`<cli> runner install` (`just runner-install`, a recipe `init` adds to the project's `justfile` when missing, pointing at the installed CLI; run by `init`, or by the owner when `init` cannot: auto mode blocks agents from installing LaunchAgents) writes `~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.filipkrawiec.workflow-runner.<owner>-<repo>.plist` and loads it with `launchctl bootstrap gui/<uid>`. The job polls every 120 s from a tooling checkout cloned once to `~/.cache/workflow-cli/skills`, which fast-forwards to the fetched default branch before each poll (a failed update is logged and the poll runs on the current checkout); logs go to `~/Library/Logs/workflow-runner/<owner>-<repo>/`. A rerun with unchanged inputs changes nothing; changed inputs rewrite and reload the job. `runner uninstall` (`just runner-uninstall`) boots it out and removes the plist; `runner status` reports `job.installed` and `job.loaded`. A `poll.lock` in the log directory keeps two simultaneous polls from both starting a session.

`issue create` is the only path to a new issue, for agents and for cloud and chat sessions: it first matches every open issue and those closed in the last 30 days (a shared scope path or none, and the same failing behaviour or outcome) and, on a match, comments the evidence, reopens a closed match and prints `Exists: #N` instead of creating. `--distinct-from N --reason "<one line>"` files a separate issue. Never use raw `gh` or GitHub tools to create one.
