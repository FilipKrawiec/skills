# Deliverable Formats

Definition, Specification, and Retrospective are inline Task values. Phase results and Phase Improvements are compact inline Task Execution values. Large documents, patches, logs, and reports are Artifact References with ID, type, revision, URI, and SHA-256.

Architectural decisions remain short ADRs under `docs/adr/`. Task briefs and plans are semantic values/artifacts, not sections of a linear SDLC record.
