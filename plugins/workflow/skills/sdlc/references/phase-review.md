# REVIEW Phase Run

REVIEW 1 is broad, independent, vigilant, and grounded in deterministic sensors. Route planning, scope, or approach defects to PLAN; route implementation-only defects to EXECUTE when the plan remains valid.

REVIEW 2 and REVIEW 3 receive the prior review, verify its findings, run regression sensors, and inspect only the delta since the previous review. New feedback must cite a defect introduced by that delta. After REVIEW 3, unresolved HIGH or CRITICAL findings reject Task Execution; lower severities become accepted risks and improvement evidence. REVIEW 4 is forbidden.
